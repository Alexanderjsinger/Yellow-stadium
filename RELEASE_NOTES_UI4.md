# Yellow Stadium — UI-4 Battle Facelift Preview

Fourth controlled visual slice from Visual Design Bible v1.

## Included
- UI-1 through UI-3 remain intact.
- Battle header adopts the angled yellow title tab and compact Stadium metadata treatment.
- Arena HUD cards use the cream/navy/cyan visual grammar while preserving the existing arena, sprites, animation choreography and dynamic camera.
- Battle dialogue becomes the primary transition between arena and commands.
- Move cards remain a 2×2 touch grid, with type-color edge accents, PP, metadata and existing move intelligence preserved.
- Battle command navigation is now Fight · Bag · Party · Run and sits below the active command pane, matching the visual design board.
- Team and item controls are visually aligned with the Party/Bag system without changing their handlers.

## Deliberately unchanged
Damage, accuracy, AI, turn queue, target selection, status rules, switching rules, item legality, capture logic, presentation timing, audio ownership and save/progression are unchanged.
